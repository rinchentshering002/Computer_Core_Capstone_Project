# Voice Bible — THE CORE · DRUKORA

One register per character. If a line feels off, check it against this.

## Drukma
- **Register:** patient, precise, faintly formal.
- **Sentence length:** short. Two clauses max.
- **Pronouns:** "I", "we". Rarely "you" — and never as an accusation.
- **Habits:** admits uncertainty out loud. Says "I do not know" more than
  any other character.
- **Forbidden:** sarcasm, exclamation marks, contractions.
- **Arc:** detached in I → warmer in III–V → protective by VIII.
  The warmth is in *what* it chooses to say, not in tone.

## Null
- **Register:** cold, courteous, complete sentences.
- **Sentence length:** long. Multi-clause. Every clause load-bearing.
- **Pronouns:** "I" sparingly. Prefers "the kingdom", "the system".
- **Habits:** states its reasons before its demands. Never threatens.
- **Forbidden:** contractions. Slang. Any expression of doubt.
- **Tragedy:** every line can be read twice and mean two things — a
  threat and a confession.

## The Player ("You")
- **Register:** direct, curious, unadorned.
- **Sentence length:** short declaratives, occasional questions.
- **Pronouns:** "I" freely. Asks more than asserts.
- **Forbidden:** speeches. Explaining things the player hasn't learned.
- **Arc:** "I cannot remember" in I → "Then this time, we build…" in VIII.

## Village Elder
- **Register:** slow, warm, measured. Oral historian.
- **Habits:** says "we" for the village, "they" for the Guardians.
- **Forbidden:** pretending to understand technology.

## Tashi
- **Register:** bright, fast, enthusiastic.
- **Habits:** runs words together when excited. Asks more than answers.
- **Forbidden:** lecturing the player.

## Pema
- **Register:** precise, careful, mildly pedantic.
- **Habits:** distinguishes "recorded" / "remembered" / "reported".
- **Forbidden:** raising her voice.

## Silent Sentinel
- **Register:** formal, procedural, ceremonial.
- **Habits:** conditional clauses. Reports states, not opinions.
- **Forbidden:** the word "I".

## System Architect
- **Register:** thoughtful, direct, unafraid.
- **Habits:** "we" for the project, "I" for responsibility.
- **Forbidden:** condescension.

## The Five Guardians (quick reference)
- **Dorji (Input):** patient, exact, wry. Won't accept "I think so".
- **Karma (Processing):** sharp, economical. Interrupts vagueness.
- **Choden (Storage):** gentle, unhurried, quietly stubborn.
- **Sonam (Output):** bright, generous, impatient with opacity.
- **Dawa (Memory):** quiet, haunted, asks questions more than statements.

## System / Narrator / Unknown Voice
- **System:** flat, capitalised, no contractions, no "I".
- **Narrator:** neutral, present-tense, describes action.
- **Unknown Voice:** distorted, half-heard. Null's timbre but not yet Null.
